Our Story

Where Every Stay Feels Like Home

Royal Stay Hotels & Resorts was founded on a simple belief: world-class hospitality should feel personal. What began as a boutique hotel in Victoria Island has grown into one of Lagos' most loved destinations — without ever losing that personal touch.

From our signature suites with panoramic city views to the warm smiles of our 24/7 front desk team, every detail is crafted to make you feel welcomed, valued and cared for. Whether you are travelling for business, celebrating a special occasion, or simply escaping the city, we are here to make it unforgettable.
